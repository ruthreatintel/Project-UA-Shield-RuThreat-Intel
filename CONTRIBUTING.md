# Contributing Guidelines

To participate in this project, you need to meet the following criteria:

* **Experience:** Some experience with OSINT and IOC collection.
* **Location Restrictions:** Not from countries such as Belarus or Russia.
* **Preferred Location:** It is preferred that you be from Ukraine (but not required).
